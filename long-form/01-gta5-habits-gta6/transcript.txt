In GTA 5, you could steal a car, turn two corners, and the police simply forgot you existed. In GTA 6, that habit is going to get you caught. Everything here is either confirmed by Rockstar, or reported by several reliable outlets, and I'll tell you which is which. The game launches on November nineteenth, so real reviews are only starting. Part two comes once they land. So here are eight GTA 5 habits you'll have to unlearn. Habit number one: the lazy getaway. Rockstar says the police can now identify you by your vehicle, your clothing, your physical description, and even CCTV footage. So the old trick, ditch the car, turn a corner, and walk away, may not work the way it used to. I say may, because Rockstar hasn't explained how fast, or how well, the police put those clues together. My plan: until I test it, I'll assume every chase ends with the cops remembering my car and my outfit. Habit number two: ignoring the stars. The six star wanted system is back, and Rockstar has added a new layer called Heat. How Heat works hasn't been explained yet, but the name alone suggests the police may remember what you did. My plan: stop treating stars as a timer, and start treating them as a record. Habit number three: treating the weather like wallpaper. Rockstar says it rebuilt the weather system from scratch, building on technology from Red Dead Redemption two. Tropical storms and hurricanes are confirmed. How often they hit, and what they change, hasn't been shown. So don't assume the sky is only decoration. Check it before a big job, and have a backup plan. Habit number four: running past the animals. Game Informer reports more than one hundred and seventy animal species, including alligators, manatees, and flamingos. More than sixty of those are birds, and wildlife is split into rare, discoverable, and legendary tiers. Rockstar hasn't said which animals sit in which tier, so that part is yours to discover. My plan: the first time I leave the city, I'm slowing down and actually looking around. Habit number five: expecting a smooth sixty frames per second. Rockstar has said GTA 6 targets thirty frames per second on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series consoles at launch. Whether a sixty frame mode ever arrives is still undecided, so if your eyes are used to smooth games, give them a little time. Habit number six: planning to jump into online with your friends. Rockstar is selling GTA 6 as a single-player story about Jason and Lucia, and has not announced an online mode or a date for it. One report says multiplayer could arrive in twenty twenty seven, but that is unconfirmed, so enjoy the story first. Habit number seven: thinking you already know the map. Leonida is reported to be about twice the size of GTA 5's map, and Rockstar has named Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers, and Mount Kalaga National Park. Outlets also report activities like kayaking, base jumping, billiards, and gym workouts, so there is more than shooting out there. My plan: pick one region, and explore it properly before racing across the whole state. Habit number eight: leaving the download for the last minute. The game needs up to two hundred and twelve gigabytes of space, depending on your device. And on PlayStation, the store listing shows the game unlocking at midnight, local time, on November nineteenth. So free up the space today, not at midnight. That's eight habits: the lazy getaway, ignoring the stars, the weather, the animals, thirty frames, no online, a huge map, and a huge download. Which of these habits is going to hurt you the most?

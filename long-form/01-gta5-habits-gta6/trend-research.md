What wins: numbered "N things" lists from the PLAYER's side (what you must do / unlearn), with a GTA 5 comparison, 8-16 min, a strong
one-word or promise title. Big channels own the generic "everything we know" and map guides; small channels get 2k-25k in the first day.
Lane for a 4-subscriber channel: the GTA 5 -> GTA 6 "habits you must unlearn" angle (proven at 2.78M by a bigger channel) done as a
confirmed-only, honestly sourced list with a clear hook ("the lazy getaway gets you caught"), at 4-5 min so retention stays high.
Chosen topic: **GTA 5 Habits That Will Get You Caught In GTA 6** (8 habits, every claim from Game Informer coverage / Rockstar / 2+ outlets;
unconfirmed items carry "reported" / "unconfirmed" on screen). Not chosen: map guide (6M, owned by a 15-minute video from a big channel),
"secrets" (needs leaks, not allowed), reviews (do not exist yet).

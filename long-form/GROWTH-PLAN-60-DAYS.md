# 60-day plan: GTA 6 launch -> monetization (written 18 Nov 2026, Kratos Playz + solo hype)

Sources are third-party blogs unless noted: check the numbers on YouTube's own Help page before acting. Nothing here is a promise of views or money.

## 1. The deadline that matters (reported)
- Today's YPP (YouTube Partner Program): 1,000 subscribers + (4,000 public long-form watch hours in 12 months OR 10M public Shorts views in 90 days) = ads.
  500-subscriber tier (reported: 3,000 hours or 3M Shorts views) = memberships / Super Thanks / Shopping affiliate only, no ads.
- **Reported: YouTube raised the bar for NEW channels from 1 Feb 2027 (8,000 hours or 20M Shorts views).** So the real target is to qualify before 31 Jan 2027 = about 10 weeks. (tella.com, air.io, ppc.land coverage)
- Math for 4,000 hours: 240,000 minutes. With 2.5 min average view on a 4-5 min video that is ~96,000 long-form views; with 6 min average on 8-10 min videos ~40,000 views. Shorts watch time does NOT count toward the 4,000 hours (Shorts count only on the 10M-views route).
- Honest odds: from 4 subscribers this is a stretch. GTA 6 launch week is the one time the search demand is huge, so we go all-in on it and judge on day 14 and day 30.

## 2. How money is actually made (ranked by speed)
1. **YPP ads**: needs the threshold. Rates are low and depend on audience country: long-form gaming about Rs 15-60 per 1,000 views for India, much higher for US/UK; Shorts about Rs 2-8 per 1,000 views in India. 100k long-form views is roughly Rs 1.5k-6k (India) and several times that for a US/UK audience. English + global audience pays more than Hindi.
2. **500-sub tier** (earlier than ads): memberships, Super Thanks, Shopping affiliate (reported opened at 500 subs in Mar 2026).
3. **Affiliate links** in descriptions (PS5 SSD / extra storage for the 212 GB install, controllers, headsets, game-pass-style stores): works without ads, needs an affiliate account (owner) and a clear "affiliate link" disclosure. We use NO game footage, so Rockstar's non-commercial fan-content rule is not triggered by our visuals.
4. **Digital product / community** with the owner's connected Superprofile: free "GTA 6 checklist" PDF -> Telegram/WhatsApp list; paid community only after ~5-10k real followers. Slow, low odds early.
5. **Sponsorships / brand deals**: only after ~1,000+ subscribers with steady views. Not a first-60-days income.
6. Instagram bonuses are invite-only and unreliable; keep Instagram as a free traffic source (Reels = same clips).

## 3. Rockstar / YouTube risk (important)
- Rockstar's fan-content policy (support.rockstargames.com) allows footage only for non-commercial use, and removes leaked / pre-release material; spoilers are case by case. Monetized GTA footage is therefore a grey area. Our drawn-graphics videos avoid this; never use leaks, never use footage in monetized videos unless Rockstar's current policy is checked.
- YouTube "inauthentic content": a repeated template with only the words changed can be demonetized. Each video needs its own research, angle and opening; vary format. A real human voice (owner) is the strongest answer to this.
- YouTube relaxed violence-in-games monetization rules before launch (Dexerto, Sep 2026): still no shock thumbnails.

## 4. The plan
**Week 1 (18-24 Nov, launch week)**: 2 long videos + 3 Shorts/day (upload quota about 5-6 per day for BOTH channels; a long upload costs the same quota as a Short).
 - Long 01 is scheduled (19 Nov 16:30 IST). Long 02 (22 Nov): "What reviews actually say" or the first confirmed-by-outlets "mistakes new players make" (only after real reviews exist; mark everything reported).
 - Shorts: same-day verified news (reviews, records, patch, price/unlock stories). No leaks, no pirated footage.
**Weeks 2-4**: 2 long videos/week (6-10 min, chapters, "N things" or "explained" titles, English), 2-3 Shorts/day. Every video ends with the NEXT video (end screen + pinned comment + playlist "GTA 6 Guide") to push session time.
 - Rule from the data: lists from the PLAYER's side (what to do / unlearn / avoid) beat news; copy the pattern, bring a new topic. Sequel to the best video within 3 days.
**Weeks 5-8**: double down on what won (topic + length); drop what lost twice. Judge: day 14 (any long video over 1,000 views? average view duration over 40%?), day 30 (subscribers, hours), day 45 (is 4,000 hours reachable by 31 Jan? if no, switch to the 500-sub tier plan and evergreen topics).
**KPIs (guesses, to be replaced by data)**: day 14: 50 subs; day 30: 200; day 60: 600-1,000 if a video breaks out.

## 5. Videos must NOT feel flat (lesson from long 01 v1)
v1 had music at -43 dB under the voice and a 3% camera move. v2 adds: hook headline slam + zoom-in, whip / glitch / flash cuts every shot, punch zoom + shake on slam sounds, light leaks, beat music (98 BPM kick, clap, hats, bass, pad), risers + booms before every habit card, louder SFX, progress bar. This is now in the `cctv-wide` style code (`src/main.js`, `tools/mix.py` music "drive", `sfx_boost`). Every new video uses it.
